The Mentor Assistance Program

MAP pairs San Diego County high school students in grades 10–12 with a UC San Diego faculty member or postdoctoral researcher for an eight-month mentorship, from fall to spring. Run by the San Diego Supercomputer Center and the UC San Diego School of Medicine, it reaches across the whole campus, from history and marine physical sciences to nanoengineering, pharmacology and computer science.

Each student sets their own goals with their mentor: a research project, a science fair entry, regular visits to the lab, or guided reading in a field they want to explore. The program ends with a final presentation.

Meet our mentor

Dr. Javier Duarte & the Duarte Lab

Dr. Duarte is an Associate Professor of Physics at UC San Diego and a member of the CMS experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider. His group develops new artificial intelligence techniques for high-energy particle collisions, to measure the properties and interactions of elementary particles like the Higgs boson more precisely and to search for new physics.
